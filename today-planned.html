Today Planned

App Store Website

Today Planned came out of a simple problem: I kept missing events on my calendar, because notifications were too easy to swipe away and forget. So I built an app that turns the events that matter into real alarms, the kind that ring even in Silent Mode or Do Not Disturb.

Over time it became where I keep the rest of my day too. Notes, links, files, and plans live alongside events in a section called Things, filters decide which events should become alarms, and a few widgets keep whatever is next in view on the Home and Lock Screen.

I design, build, and ship it on my own, across iPhone and Apple Watch. It is written in SwiftUI with SwiftData, and uses widgets, Live Activities, and App Intents to show up across the system. It is localized in 11 languages, and most of the release work runs automatically: translation, screenshots, and delivery through Xcode Cloud.
